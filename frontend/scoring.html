Multiply this rate by the pick’s Classic points to get its fixed Upset Bonus.

Every correct pick earns its Classic points plus a bonus. Lower projected win totals earn larger bonuses; favorites earn smaller bonuses.

Minnesota at 7.5 wins: a correct Wild Card pick earns 5 Classic + 6.18 bonus = 11.18 points. Everyone who makes that pick earns exactly the same amount, regardless of their other picks.

Classic remains out of 300. Upset Edge total is Classic + Upset Bonus and can exceed 300. For example, 247 Classic + 231.40 bonus = 478.40 Upset Edge points. No bracket normalization or shared point budgets.

Each pick’s bonus is rounded to the nearest hundredth before adding it to the score. Incorrect or missing picks earn zero. Later-round credit still follows the team advancing, even if you missed its matchup.

Values use the app’s fixed 2026 FanDuel/DraftKings win-total snapshot from VegasInsider. They are set before the season; live odds and other brackets never change a pick’s value.

Both totals appear on the leaderboards. Public standings show both scores together, while groups keep the scoring option chosen at creation. Existing groups use Classic.
