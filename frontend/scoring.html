More credit for surprises.

Multiplier = 18 − the team’s preseason Vegas win total. Lower-projected teams carry more weight in both seeding and playoffs.

A team projected for 6.5 wins gets an 11.5 multiplier. A team projected for 11.5 wins gets a 6.5 multiplier.

Points per pick = 150 × its weight ÷ the total weight of all your picks in that phase. The table shows weights, not fixed point awards. Field, division and seed picks share 150 points; playoff picks share another 150.

Points are rounded to hundredths, with leftover hundredths assigned to the largest remainders. Missing picks reserve points and earn zero. A perfect bracket earns exactly 300.

Weights use the app’s fixed 2026 FanDuel/DraftKings win-total snapshot from VegasInsider. Live line changes never change these weights. Your allocation depends on your full bracket and freezes when picks lock.

Later-round credit follows the team advancing, even if you missed its matchup.
