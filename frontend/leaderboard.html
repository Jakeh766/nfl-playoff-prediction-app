Understanding the standings

The public leaderboard compares saved playoff predictions. NFL standings are the default; view NBA standings to compare basketball brackets. Scores update as season results become available.

Classic totals use fixed point awards. Upset Edge uses each team’s frozen preseason win-total weight. Select a player to view their public bracket, or sign in to view your private groups. Account details stay private.

Read the scoring rules for both sports or build your prediction. Standings load with JavaScript; they are not editorial rankings or forecasts of which teams will win.
