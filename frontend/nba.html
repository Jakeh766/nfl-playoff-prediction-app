About Predict Playoffs

What is Predict Playoffs?

Predict Playoffs is an independent web app for NFL and NBA playoff predictions. Build a preseason bracket, save one prediction per sport with your account, and compare your picks with other players.

How does the NBA predictor work?

Predict the final eight playoff teams in each conference after the Play-In: 16 teams total. Seed the East and West from 1 to 8, then choose each series winner through the NBA Finals. Play-In games, individual games, and series lengths are not scored.

When do predictions lock?

The season deadline is October 20, 2026 at 19:00 UTC. Save your bracket before that deadline. Once predictions lock, you cannot create or change a saved bracket.

How are Classic and Upset Edge different?

Classic awards points for correct playoff teams, exact seeds, and teams advancing, with a perfect score of 300. Upset Edge multiplies those awards by each team’s fixed preseason win-total weight, giving more weight to lower projected win totals. Read the NBA scoring rules.

Can I compete with friends?

Yes. Create or join a private group and compare saved predictions. A group uses its chosen scoring mode, while the public leaderboard lets you compare Classic and Upset Edge. View NBA standings or make your picks.

Which sports are supported?

Predict Playoffs supports both football and basketball, with separate brackets and scores. Try the NFL playoff predictor. Predict Playoffs is not affiliated with the NFL or NBA.
