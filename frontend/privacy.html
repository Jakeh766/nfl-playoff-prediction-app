Privacy policy

Updated October 6, 2026

This policy explains what Predict Playoffs collects and how you can manage your information.

Information we collect

We collect your email and account information, saved predictions and brackets, leaderboard name, scores and standings, and groups and group memberships. We also collect basic technical information, such as browser information, requests, and errors, to operate and protect the site.

How we use information

We use information to operate accounts, sign you in and help recover your account, save predictions, run leaderboards and groups, send account-related emails, secure and maintain the service, and improve Predict Playoffs.

We do not sell your personal information.

Analytics and browser storage

We use essential Cognito browser storage to keep you signed in and maintain your session. Analytics do not create cookies or use localStorage or sessionStorage. We do not use Google Analytics (GA4) or Microsoft Clarity.

We use cookieless traffic analytics to understand visits and navigation between public pages. It may receive the public page visited, referring site when available, screen width, browser and language information, and IP address, and derive approximate location. Shared picks, leaderboard, and scoring pages are labelled only NFL or NBA. We exclude raw query strings, URL fragments, account information, invite codes, and individual bracket selections.

We also collect aggregate counts of actions such as account creation and deletion, sign-ins, bracket activity, and group activity. These counts exclude account information, IP addresses, browser identifiers, invite codes, and individual picks.

GoatCounter temporarily links a cookieless session identifier to the site, IP address and browser User-Agent in memory for up to eight hours. This estimates short-lived sessions rather than identifying returning people.

Google Search Console provides aggregate information about our appearance in Google Search, including clicks, impressions, queries, pages, countries and devices. It does not add a tracking script or browser storage to this site.

On public development pages, active-time measurement starts automatically unless Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track is enabled. We estimate time while the tab is visible and focused, pausing after one minute without a click, key press, scroll or touch. We observe only whether an interaction occurred, never typed text, pointer coordinates or recordings.

Active-time measurement sends only a fixed public page label, NFL/NBA when relevant, and a short elapsed-time interval to our first-party AWS endpoint. We store daily aggregate totals by page and sport, with no visitor identifiers or individual activity records. Network and security systems still process normal request information. Measurement ends when you leave the page and resumes on a new visit or when the page is restored from browser history. It creates no cookies or browser storage and has no consent banner or opt-in control.

We honor supported Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals by disabling traffic, product and active-time analytics, including when a signal is enabled after page load. Essential authentication and security functions stay active.

Service providers

We use third-party service providers for hosting, storage, authentication, email delivery, security, monitoring, and traffic analytics.

Public and group information

Your leaderboard name, scores, and saved brackets may be publicly visible where applicable. Private group information, including standings and past results, is visible to group members. Avoid putting personal information in leaderboard or group names.

Data retention and account deletion

We keep account and application information as needed to provide the service. Analytics, security and operational records, and backups may have different retention periods.

Active-time daily totals expire approximately thirteen months after their reporting month. AWS may take additional time to remove expired records.

You can update your leaderboard name or delete your account through Account. Deleting your account removes saved predictions and brackets, your current leaderboard information, and group memberships. If you manage a group, first appoint a new commissioner and leave the group.

Past group results may retain your leaderboard name and scores for group members. Limited copies of other information may remain temporarily in backups, security records, or operational logs.

Contact

For questions or requests about this policy or your information, contact us at contact@predictplayoffs.com.
