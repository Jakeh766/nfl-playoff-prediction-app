Privacy policy

Updated October 3, 2026

This policy explains what Predict Playoffs collects and how you can manage your information.

Information we collect

We collect your email and account information, saved predictions and brackets, leaderboard name, scores and standings, and groups and group memberships. We also collect basic technical information, such as browser information, requests, and errors, to operate and protect the site.

How we use information

We use information to operate accounts, sign you in and help recover your account, save predictions, run leaderboards and groups, send account-related emails, secure and maintain the service, and improve Predict Playoffs.

We do not sell your personal information.

Analytics, cookies, and browser storage

We use essential browser storage to keep you signed in and remember your preferences. Optional analytics use cookies and similar technologies only after you choose Allow analytics. If you do nothing, optional analytics stay off.

You can choose Decline analytics and still use the site. Change your choice through Cookie preferences in the footer. When you decline, we turn off optional analytics and remove their cookies and stored visitor/session identifiers.

We may collect limited aggregate usage counts without optional cookies, including when you decline. These measurements are not designed to identify individual visitors. We honor supported Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals by disabling optional analytics and our own usage measurements.

Our analytics are configured not to send email addresses, account identifiers, invite codes, and individual bracket selections. Google advertising personalization is disabled.

Service providers

We use third-party service providers for hosting, data storage, authentication, email delivery, security, and monitoring.

With your permission, Google Analytics 4 measures site traffic and actions, and Microsoft Clarity provides heatmaps and masked session recordings. These analytics services receive basic information about your browser and visit.

Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services and read the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Public and group information

Your leaderboard name, scores, and saved brackets may be publicly visible where applicable. Private group information, including standings and past results, is visible to group members. Avoid putting personal information in leaderboard or group names.

Data retention and account deletion

We keep account and application information as needed to provide the service. Analytics, security and operational records, and backups may have different retention periods.

You can update your leaderboard name or delete your account through Account. Deleting your account removes saved predictions and brackets, your current leaderboard information, and group memberships. If you manage a group, first appoint a new commissioner and leave the group.

Past group results may retain your leaderboard name and scores for group members. Limited copies of other information may remain temporarily in backups, security records, or operational logs.

Contact

For questions or requests about this policy or your information, contact us at contact@predictplayoffs.com.
