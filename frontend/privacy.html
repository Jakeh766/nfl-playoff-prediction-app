Privacy policy

Updated September 28, 2026

This policy explains what Predict Playoffs collects and how you can manage your information.

Information we collect

We collect your email and account information, saved predictions and brackets, leaderboard name, scores and standings, and groups and group memberships. We also collect basic technical information, such as browser information, requests, and errors, to operate and protect the site.

Your leaderboard name, scores, and saved brackets may be publicly visible where applicable. Private group information, including standings and past results, is visible to group members. Avoid putting personal information in leaderboard or group names.

How we use information

We use information to operate accounts, sign you in and help recover your account, save predictions, run leaderboards and groups, send account-related emails, secure and maintain the service, and improve Predict Playoffs.

We do not sell your personal information.

Analytics

We use optional analytics to understand how people use Predict Playoffs. Google Analytics 4 measures site traffic and actions taken on the site. Microsoft Clarity provides heatmaps and masked session recordings that help us understand how visitors interact with pages.

These services load only after you choose Allow analytics. If you do nothing, they stay disabled. They receive basic information about your browser and visit.

Our analytics implementation is designed not to intentionally send sensitive information such as email addresses, account identifiers, invite codes, or individual bracket selections. We mask page content for recordings and skip recordings on visits with potentially sensitive links. Google advertising personalization is not enabled.

Our own basic usage measurements follow your analytics choice where this choice is offered. Where it is not offered, we still collect basic usage measurements.

Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services and read the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Cookies and browser storage

The site uses browser storage for sign-in and your analytics preference. Analytics providers may use cookies or browser storage after you allow analytics.

You can decline analytics and still use the site, change your choice through Analytics settings in the footer, or clear cookies and site data in your browser. We honor supported Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals by disabling optional analytics and our own usage measurements.

Service providers

Third-party service providers help us with hosting, authentication, email delivery, security and monitoring, and analytics. Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity handle analytics information as described in their policies linked above.

Data retention and account deletion

We keep account and application information as needed to provide the service. Analytics, security and operational records, and backups may have different retention periods.

You can delete your account through Account. This removes saved predictions and brackets, your current leaderboard information, and group memberships. If you manage a group, first appoint a new commissioner and leave the group.

Past group results may retain your leaderboard name and scores for group members. Limited copies of other information may remain temporarily in backups, security records, or operational logs.

Children's privacy

Predict Playoffs is a general-audience service and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You can manage your leaderboard name and delete your account through Account.

Changes

We may update this policy as Predict Playoffs changes. The updated date will appear at the top.
