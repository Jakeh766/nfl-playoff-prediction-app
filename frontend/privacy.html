Privacy policy

Updated October 5, 2026

This policy explains what Predict Playoffs collects and how you can manage your information.

Information we collect

We collect your email and account information, saved predictions and brackets, leaderboard name, scores and standings, and groups and group memberships. We also collect basic technical information, such as browser information, requests, and errors, to operate and protect the site.

How we use information

We use information to operate accounts, sign you in and help recover your account, save predictions, run leaderboards and groups, send account-related emails, secure and maintain the service, and improve Predict Playoffs.

We do not sell your personal information.

Analytics and essential browser storage

We do not use Google Analytics 4 (GA4), Microsoft Clarity, analytics cookies, or analytics localStorage/sessionStorage. There is no analytics consent banner or stored analytics consent choice. Public page loads remove legacy analytics cookies and stored analytics identifiers or choices from earlier versions without changing essential sign-in storage.

Amazon Cognito uses essential browser storage for your sign-in session, including tokens needed to keep you signed in and refresh your session. This authentication storage is unchanged. The app may also store your preferences and draft bracket to support its features; these are not analytics identifiers.

We honor supported Global Privacy Control (GPC) and Do Not Track (DNT) signals by disabling GoatCounter and our first-party product analytics collection. These signals do not disable essential authentication or security operations. Search Console reports aggregate Google Search results rather than collecting visits in your browser.

Cookieless traffic analytics

Our development site uses GoatCounter for traffic measurement. It receives an allowlisted public page path, a fixed title, the referring site's origin when available, screen width, and bot indicators. Query strings, URL fragments, invite codes, account details, and bracket selections are excluded. GoatCounter also receives the IP address and browser headers needed to process its network request and can derive browser, operating system, approximate location, and language statistics.

GoatCounter does not set analytics cookies or store visitor identifiers on your device. Its cookieless session model temporarily maps the site, IP address, and browser User-Agent to a random session identifier in memory for up to eight hours. IP addresses and full User-Agent headers are not stored in its analytics database. The session estimate is not a permanent person identifier; shared networks and matching browsers can merge visitors, while network or browser changes can count someone again.

With Individual pageviews enabled, GoatCounter retains pageview records with random session identifiers according to the site's retention settings. The eight-hour limit applies to the temporary identifying link, not deletion of historical pageviews. Our private dashboard processes exports in server memory to calculate distinct sessions, pageviews, daily trends, page totals, and estimated elapsed session duration. Raw session identifiers are never sent to the dashboard browser or written to our logs or report cache. Time after the last recorded pageview cannot be measured. Learn more in GoatCounter's privacy policy.

First-party product analytics and Google Search

We use AWS to count sign-ins, accounts created and deleted, brackets created, completed and saved by type (NFL or NBA), groups created, and group joins including invite joins. Only fixed event names, allowlisted public page paths, environment, and bracket type are retained for these measurements. We do not attach account IDs, emails, IP addresses, browser identifiers, invite codes, or individual picks. The dashboard shows aggregate daily counts and totals. These are browser-reported actions and may undercount because of privacy signals, blockers, or network failures; they are not authoritative account database totals.

Google Search Console supplies aggregate clicks, impressions, click-through rate, average position, daily history, and breakdowns by search query, page, country, and device for the connected predictplayoffs.com property. We read these performance reports on the server; no Google tracking script, analytics cookie, or localStorage is added to the site. Google may omit anonymized queries and delay recent reports.

Service providers

We use AWS and other providers for hosting, data storage, authentication, email delivery, security, and monitoring, alongside GoatCounter and Google Search Console for the measurements described above.

Public and group information

Your leaderboard name, scores, and saved brackets may be publicly visible where applicable. Private group information, including standings and past results, is visible to group members. Avoid putting personal information in leaderboard or group names.

Data retention and account deletion

We keep account and application information as needed to provide the service. Analytics, security and operational records, and backups may have different retention periods.

You can update your leaderboard name or delete your account through Account. Deleting your account removes saved predictions and brackets, your current leaderboard information, and group memberships. If you manage a group, first appoint a new commissioner and leave the group.

Past group results may retain your leaderboard name and scores for group members. Limited copies of other information may remain temporarily in backups, security records, or operational logs.

Contact

For questions or requests about this policy or your information, contact us at contact@predictplayoffs.com.
