Privacy policy

Updated September 28, 2026. This policy explains how Predict Playoffs uses account information, predictions, and analytics.

Your account and predictions

We use your email address to manage sign-in, verification, and account recovery through Amazon Cognito and our email service. We store your predictions, leaderboard name, and group memberships so you can save brackets and compete with other players.

Your leaderboard name, scores, and saved bracket may be visible on the public leaderboard. Private group standings are available to group members. Keep personal information out of your leaderboard name and group name.

Site analytics

When you allow analytics, we use Google Analytics 4 to measure visits and actions such as creating an account, building and saving a bracket, and joining a group. We use Microsoft Clarity for masked session recordings, heatmaps, and click patterns that help us improve the site. These services receive technical information about your browser and visit and may use cookies.

Our analytics events do not include your email address, Cognito account ID, group IDs, invite codes, or bracket picks. Page URLs sent to Google exclude query strings and fragments. Clarity uses strict masking, and recordings are disabled for visits with potentially sensitive URL or referrer parameters. We do not enable advertising personalization in our analytics integration.

Our first-party analytics, hosted on AWS, measure page views and successful account, prediction, and group actions using random visitor and session IDs stored in your browser. When third-party analytics is configured, the same analytics choice controls this first-party tracking.

Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services and read the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Your analytics choice

Optional Google Analytics and Clarity scripts load only after you select Allow analytics. You can decline and continue using the site. Use Analytics settings in the footer to change your choice. Declining stops optional tracking, removes the site's analytics cookies and first-party analytics IDs, and reloads the page if tracking was already active. Your preference is saved in this browser.

We honor Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals by disabling site analytics. Sign-in and saved predictions work independently of analytics. Authentication storage and operational AWS request and error monitoring remain necessary for the service to work.

Account controls and service providers

Open Account to delete your account, saved predictions, leaderboard profile, and group memberships. Your browser's storage controls can also remove locally stored sign-in and analytics preferences. Analytics and operational records are managed separately by the relevant service.

We use AWS to host and secure the application and its data, and an email delivery provider for verification and recovery messages. Google and Microsoft process analytics data according to their policies. We may update this policy as the site changes.
